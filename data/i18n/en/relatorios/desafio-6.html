6. Observed symptom

  • Common to both devices (network): the emergency PDU session on 5G gets DL NAS Transport · 5GMM cause 90 (Payload was not forwarded) at T +0.55 s, with no RRCRelease with redirection and no mobilityFromNRCommand: Zenith does not perform the expected EPS Fallback.
  • Polaris (deviation 1): instead of going to its own operator's LTE, it runs an NR + LTE band scan and only decides at T +1.20 s (Equinox: 0.65 s).
  • Polaris (deviation 2): with Zenith LTE PCI 220 (−85 dBm) on the list, it picks Aurora LTE PCI 145 (−80 dBm) by signal level. On Aurora it enters limited service (EPS emergency attach), gets 403 · Emergency registration not permitted from the IMS and completes via CSFB on Aurora's 2G (Connect at 3.72–3.95 s).
  • Equinox: goes to Zenith's LTE PCI 220, does a subscriber TAU (mapped GUTI), the emergency PDN (APN sos) and VoLTE to urn:service:sos in 1.88–2.05 s.

9. Field tester comments

The initial failure is the network's and hits both devices alike: Zenith returns cause 90 to the emergency PDU session and does not move the UE to LTE. The difference is in the UE's reaction. The Equinox tries another RAT of its own HPLMN, as the emergency network-selection hierarchy requires; the Polaris runs a wide band scan and picks another operator by signal level, even with the HPLMN's LTE on the list. The 4G control (1.62–1.70 s, equivalent to the CDUT) rules out a VoLTE, IMS, SIM or coverage defect in the Polaris: the problem only shows at the point where the device must choose the next network.

Open hypotheses: cause 90 and the missing EPS Fallback depend on interfaces outside the UE log (N11 between AMF and SMF, N2); the Polaris's internal selection criterion (operator configuration, emergency policy) does not appear in the log; with three repetitions per scenario the result is systematic, but the sample is small.

Conclusion: root cause in the DUT — after the emergency failure on 5G, the Polaris's emergency manager selects the cell by signal level across PLMNs instead of exhausting the HPLMN's RATs before trying another operator. That leads it to limited service on Aurora, to an IMS that refuses the emergency registration and to CS as the last resort, almost doubling the setup time. Recommendations: fix the selection order (HPLMN and its RATs before any other PLMN; signal only decides within the same PLMN); repeat with Aurora off air or with its signal below Zenith's; get Zenith core traces for cause 90; ≥ 20 repetitions per scenario.
