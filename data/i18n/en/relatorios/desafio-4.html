6. Observed symptom

  • TC#1: the DUT answers the 401 with a cached nonce (N0-000000-init, then N1 when the challenge is already N2) and receives 403 Forbidden with Warning: 399 "nonce does not match latest challenge" at T +0.104–0.116 s and T +0.212–0.231 s. The client abandons registration (max_auth_attempts = 2) and Voice Domain Selection leaves the UE in 5G SA without voice: no paging or INVITE arrives.
  • TC#2: the network answers the DUT's offer with a=rtpmap:97 AMR/8000, while the DUT keeps 97 = EVS/16000 (re-offered in the UPDATE) and starts the EVS 24.4 kbps vocoder on PT 97. Signalling completes without errors and RTP flows (TX 500 / RX 497–499), but voice is unintelligible.
  • CDUT: registers on the 1st attempt (200 OK in 0.098–0.109 s), receives the MT call over VoNR and, on VoLTE, uses AMR-NB on PT 97, consistent with the SDP, with clear audio.

9. Field tester comments

The two symptoms have independent causes that merely coexist in the same IMS client (Vanguard-IMS/1.0). The registration defect only appears when the nonce cache survives (airplane mode toggle); after a power cycle (TC#2) registration passes first time, yet voice still fails. The media defect only appears when the DUT is the offerer and the answer binds a different codec to PT 97; in TC#1 the UE never gets as far as negotiating media.

Ruled-out hypotheses: coverage (RSRP −82/−84 dBm, stationary UE, same cell as the CDUT); credentials (the same SIM registers first time in TC#2 with the right nonce); network/IMS (the CDUT registers and completes the calls on the same site and realm); transport/QoS (5QI/QCI 1 bearer active and RTP counters equivalent to the CDUT).

Conclusion: (1) the IMS client builds the Authorization from the cached nonce instead of the nonce in the latest 401, and the cache is not cleared when IMS restarts through airplane mode; (2) the IMS client does not apply the SDP answer's a=rtpmap and keeps its local map PT 97 = EVS, contrary to the rule that a dynamic payload type mapping must not change during the session (RFC 3264 §8.3.2). To close each cause: repeat TC#1 alternating toggle and power cycle (≥ 20 times each) and with IMS-AKA; capture the network side (P-CSCF/S-CSCF and RTP at the SBC) to confirm the payload actually sent; test TC#2 with a DUT offer that already uses 97 = AMR, and with a fixed IMS client build.
