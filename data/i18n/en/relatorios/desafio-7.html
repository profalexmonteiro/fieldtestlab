Issue Report — Field Test

Challenge 7 · S07 · 5G SA UDP throughput · Generated on {{data}}

1. Identification

Title. Vertex fails the single-carrier throughput test in downlink and uplink. Its means are 38.98% and 49.90% below the reference, respectively.

Impact. This blocks performance approval for the pre-production unit. Uplink also fails the stability criterion. Sessions remained active.

2. Device configuration

Vertex (DUT, pre-production) and Lumen (CDUT, approved). Software and modem versions are unspecified. The devices' diagnostic ports capture RRC, NAS, L1/MAC and application events.

3. Test environment and scenario

NovaLink Telecom (PLMN 724/088), 5G SA n78, PCI 214, NR-ARFCN 633312, 100 MHz, 30 kHz SCS and TDD DDDSUUDDDD. Single carrier with no CA or NR-DC. Both devices use single-layer uplink. Devices are stationary and in line of sight at a near-cell test point. Neighbor PCI 217 is on the same frequency.

4. Steps to reproduce

  1. Use the same test point and operator server for 15 runs of 60 s per device. Alternate device order for each pair, running Lumen first on odd iterations.
  2. Offer 1,500 Mbps DL and 300 Mbps UL concurrently over iperf3 UDP using 1,400-byte datagrams.
  3. Retain pairs that stayed on the test cell for the whole run, with SS-RSRP > −85 dBm, SS-SINR > 10 dB, device differences of at most 3 dB for both measurements, and no handover, RLF or session drop. At least 10 valid pairs are required.
  4. Compute the mean and sample standard deviation for each device and direction, using the CDUT as the denominator for deltas. The absolute deltas must be below 10% for the mean and below 20% for standard deviation in both directions.

6. Symptoms in iteration 11

Both devices stayed on PCI 214 under comparable RF conditions (Vertex −78.6 dBm and 24.4 dB, Lumen −79.0 dBm and 24.8 dB). Vertex delivered 571.4 / 28.0 Mbps in DL / UL versus Lumen's 952.4 / 74.0 Mbps. The offered load exceeded measured capacity. Interpret UDP loss alongside delivered throughput.

9. Diagnosis and confirmation

DL. The evidence points to the two-layer limit advertised by Vertex. RF, resource allocation and BLER make coverage or scheduling limits unlikely. The UE log cannot distinguish capability-profile, software and RX-chain causes. Inspect the enabled profile and RX chains, repeat under controlled RF, and verify that the unit advertises and uses four layers where the hardware supports them.

UL. The evidence points to inconsistent retransmission handling by Vertex's HARQ entity. Observed grants, MCS, buffer and power make starvation of radio resources, data or power unlikely. In a controlled environment, synchronize UE and gNB traces by slot and HARQ ID, verify NDI/RV state and TB combining at the receiver, and rerun with the modem fix. The gNB receiver's contribution and the exact internal trigger remain open without gNB records.

Why the session stays up. DL reception continues with two layers. The uplink issue affects a portion of data TBs and reduces useful delivery. The traces show the control link and session still active without an RLF declaration.
