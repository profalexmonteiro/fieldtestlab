9. Field tester comments

Acquisition and the RRC connection do not explain the rejections: in the Aurora log, attempts 1 and 2 detect valid cells (MIB/SIB1), complete RRC Setup and send NAS. The divergence happens in the Registration Request / NAS response. On attempt 2, the selected cell already advertises PLMN 724/078, but the request still carries SUCI Home-Network-Id 724/085; on the third attempt, the SUCI changes to 724/078 and the network authenticates, activates security and accepts the registration.

The repetition on Nimbus and the adequate signal lower the likelihood of a single cell/coverage failure; Aurora's own LTE success and the successful Boreal on the same PLMN/PCI lower the likelihood of a general network or site failure. The results point to a problem in how Aurora handles/updates the subscriber identity or profile state during the operator transition in 5G SA.

Preliminary conclusion: the traceable evidence is the mismatch between the selected PLMN (724/078) and the Home-Network-Id of the transmitted SUCI (724/085) on the second attempt, followed by the SUCI correction and success on the third. The logs do not reveal why the terminal still uses the previous identity, nor do they allow distinguishing a provisioning defect from UE state/cache. Confirm by correlating IMSI/SUCI in a protected way, eSIM/pSIM state and core network responses; repeat with NAS capture and controlled profiles, without exposing subscriber identifiers.
