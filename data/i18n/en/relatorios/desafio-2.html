9. Field tester comments

The contrast between Meridian's intra-frequency control and the successful inter-frequency CDUT localizes the deviation in the DUT's inter-frequency behaviour, with no evidence of widespread RLF or a general site failure. The data point to a delay in the measurement/handover decision path and a post-RLF access attempt on PCI 88 even though it fails the S criterion.

Preliminary conclusion: the evidence is consistent with an anomaly in Meridian's inter-frequency measurement processing/triggering and in its post-RLF cell selection. The exact root cause cannot be closed with these UE logs alone: Xn/NGAP signalling, additional repeated samples, detailed RF values over time and confirmation of the internal eligibility decision for PCI 88 are missing. Correlating these data and repeating the test after a modem/software fix is recommended.
