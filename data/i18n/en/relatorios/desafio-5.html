3. Test environment and scenario

Network / operator: Zenith Telecom, PLMN 724/078, commercial network.

Cells: origin site with n78 · NR-ARFCN 633120 · PCI 88 (no VoNR) and B3 · EARFCN 1444 · PCI 220, co-located. SRVCC neighbour in the far-cell: GERAN 900 · ARFCN 62 · NCC 3 / BCC 5. No usable UTRAN neighbour on that stretch.

Route / condition: mobility route with marginal 5G SA/LTE coverage, the same for DUT and CDUT; the LTE serving drops from −90 dBm to −116 dBm on reaching the far-cell.

4. Steps to reproduce

  1. With the device on 5G SA (PCI 88) and the IMS PDU session active, place (MO) or receive (MT) a voice call.
  2. Check the EPS Fallback (RRCRelease with redirectedCarrierInfo → EARFCN 1444), the TAU and the QCI 1 dedicated bearer activation; measure the Call Setup Time from INVITE (MO) or Paging (MT) to the SIP 200 OK.
  3. Compare the EPSBearerIdentity of the Activate dedicated EPS bearer context accept with the request's.
  4. With the call active, follow the route to the far-cell and observe event B2 (GERAN ARFCN 62), the mobilityFromEUTRACommand and the outcome.
  5. Compare the DUT's and CDUT's TAU Request IE by IE. Repeat 3 times per scenario.

6. Observed symptom

  • Symptom 1 (Call Setup Time): the dedicated bearer request carries EBI 6 / Linked EBI 5; the Polaris's first accept answers EPSBearerIdentity 5. The network only considers the bearer active after retransmitting the request on T3485 (8.0 s later), when the Polaris answers EBI 6. Mean CST: 11.35 s (MO) and 11.55 s (MT), against 3.39 s and 3.51 s on the CDUT (Δ −234.7 % and −228.8 %).
  • Symptom 2 (far-cell drop): the Polaris sends three B2 Measurement Reports (PCell −108/−111/−114 dBm, GERAN ARFCN 62 at −75 dBm) and receives no mobilityFromEUTRACommand; T310 expires, RLF follows, re-establishment fails and the call drops 21.30–21.51 s after the 1st report, with no SIP BYE.
  • CDUT: accept with EBI 6 on the 1st attempt and, with the same B2 report, it receives the SRVCC command; RR Handover Complete 2.05–2.22 s after the report and the call continues in CS (FR AMR).

9. Field tester comments

There are two independent causes in the same call. For symptom 1, network, radio and call direction are ruled out: same site and same MME for the CDUT, RACH on the 1st attempt, RSRP −90 dBm, identical pattern in MO and MT. The delay matches T3485, and the only difference is the accept's EPSBearerIdentity. For symptom 2, radio conditions and UE measurement are ruled out: both devices send the same B2 report. The difference is in the TAU: without MS Classmark 2/3 and Supported Codecs, the network lacks what the MSC needs to prepare the call in CS, and SRVCC is never triggered.

Hypotheses still open: the UE log shows neither the MME (discarding the accept, the "SRVCC operation possible" indication to the eNB) nor the Sv/MSC interface; nor does it show the DUT SIM's subscription data in the HSS (STN-SR, C-MSISDN), which could prevent SRVCC on its own.

Conclusion: (1) the Polaris answers the Activate dedicated EPS bearer context request with the Linked EBI instead of the bearer's EBI (TS 24.301 §6.4.2); the network ignores the accept and only confirms the bearer on the T3485 retransmission, adding ~8 s to the Call Setup Time; (2) the Polaris declares SRVCC capability but omits MS Classmark 2, MS Classmark 3 and Supported Codecs in the TAU (TS 24.301 §5.5.3.2.2), preventing SRVCC preparation and dropping the call in the far-cell. To close: MME trace (S1AP and Sv) on the same calls; swap SIMs between DUT and CDUT; a fixed Polaris build; ≥ 20 repetitions per scenario.
